## About me

I am a **computer science and mathematics educator** with a background in **software development, mathematical modelling, and artificial intelligence**. My work spans classroom teaching, university-level laboratory instruction, research-oriented computing, and tools that make everyday development more practical.

I hold bachelor's degrees in **Computer Science** and **Mathematics** from the **West University of Timișoara (UVT)**, where I also pursued postgraduate studies in **Artificial Intelligence and Distributed Computing**.

My interests lie at the intersection of **rigorous mathematics and usable software**: from probability and numerical methods to machine learning, algorithms, and Linux-based development workflows.

### Selected academic project

**Nonlinear Dynamics Approximation Using Dynamic Mode Decomposition and Autoencoders**  
Research exploring how **autoencoders** and **Dynamic Mode Decomposition** can be combined to approximate nonlinear dynamical behaviour from trajectory data, including experiments with fractal-like systems and bounded-versus-divergent trajectories.

The project brings together mathematical modelling, machine learning, numerical experiments and scientific programming.
